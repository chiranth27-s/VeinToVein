Universal Donor

O− red cells carry no A, B or RhD antigens — they can be transfused into any ABO/Rh patient in an emergency.

Can Donate To

Can Receive From

Compatibility is governed by ABO and Rh(D) antigens on red-cell surfaces. In critical shortages, hospitals prioritise O− and O+ donors first. Whole blood may be donated once every 90 days; platelets every 14 days.
