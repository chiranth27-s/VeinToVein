DONOR WELLNESS

Eligibility guide

General information to help you prepare before contacting a blood bank.

✓

Before you donate

  • Carry valid identification and answer the screening questions honestly.
  • Have a meal and stay well hydrated, following the blood bank's instructions.
  • Tell staff about medicines, recent illness, travel and medical history.
  • Follow the blood bank's advice on donation intervals.
!

Important to know

  • Age, weight, haemoglobin, health conditions and other criteria may affect eligibility.
  • Eligibility rules vary by country and blood bank.
  • Do not donate if you feel unwell; contact a qualified professional.
  • Only trained staff can confirm whether you can donate today.
⚕
Medical disclaimer

VeinToVein is a student project prototype. This checklist is not a diagnosis, medical advice, or eligibility verification. Do not use it to decide whether to donate.
